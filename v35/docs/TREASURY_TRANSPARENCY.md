# TROLL NFT 2.0 — V35 Treasury Transparency

V35 defines the business-accounting boundary for an honest long-term project.

## Revenue
Only realized, evidenced business inflows may be called revenue:
- primary mint revenue;
- royalties actually received;
- grants actually received;
- B2B/integration revenue;
- subscription/analytics revenue.

## Costs
Tracked separately:
- security/audit;
- infrastructure;
- legal/compliance;
- product/development;
- reward funding;
- other operating costs.

## Never call these revenue
- NFT floor value;
- nominal value of NFT-bound assets;
- unrealized token/stock gains;
- wash volume;
- self-transfers;
- community trading volume.

## Reward funding
Funding a reward epoch is a project cost/allocation, not revenue and not proof of future distributions.

## Exact-unit accounting
Different assets/chains are never collapsed into one total without a separate, timestamped valuation layer.

## Transparency dashboard
A future public dashboard can publish:
- grants received;
- realized protocol/business revenue;
- reward funding;
- security spend;
- infrastructure spend;
- operating costs;
- verified evidence references.

Private operational data and personal information should not be exposed.

## Goal
Make it possible for collectors, partners and grant programs to distinguish:
**project revenue, NFT wallet assets, holder assets, reward funding, and market prices.**
