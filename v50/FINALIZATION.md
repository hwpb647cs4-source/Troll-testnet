# TROLL NFT 2.0 — FINALIZATION

## Product scope is now frozen

No more feature layers before launch readiness.

The product is intentionally simple at the holder level:

1. **5,000 permanent NFTs**
2. **Burn TROLL → evolve the NFT**
3. **Higher evolution → higher configured reward weight**
4. **Each NFT has a persistent wallet/vault**
5. **Approved rewards/entitlements accumulate with verifiable evidence**
6. **When the NFT transfers, its identity, evolution and history remain attached to the token ID**
7. **The Value Passport shows the history clearly**

Everything else in the repository exists to support security, verification, analytics, operations or integration. It is not another holder mechanic.
