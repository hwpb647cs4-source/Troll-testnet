# TROLL Growth Flywheel Constitution

The business can grow aggressively without depending on deception or manufactured market pressure.

## Flywheel

**Useful NFT**
→ attracts collectors/builders
→ more protocol activity
→ more verified fees/revenue/grants
→ more product development and permitted rewards
→ stronger passports/vault biographies
→ more utility and collector interest
→ more useful NFT

The loop can repeat indefinitely, but every cycle must be grounded in real activity.

## Permanent rules

1. **No guaranteed appreciation.**
2. **No guaranteed yield.**
3. **No wash trading or fake volume.**
4. **No undisclosed insider dumping.**
5. **No manufactured or coordinated market squeeze as a product requirement.**
6. **No fake vault balances, fake stock ownership or symbol-only asset claims.**
7. **No use of customer/private wallet keys.**
8. **No reward routing into unverified contracts.**
9. **No hiding security findings.**
10. **No mainnet shortcut around the release gate.**

## What we optimize instead

- real users;
- repeat usage;
- verified wallet accumulation;
- transparent protocol revenue;
- grant funding;
- partnerships;
- developer integrations;
- useful agent tooling;
- international distribution;
- secondary-market discoverability;
- collector trust;
- security.

## Revenue can fund growth

Potential business revenue:
- primary mint revenue;
- clearly disclosed creator royalties where supported;
- premium analytics/passport tools;
- agent tooling/subscriptions;
- B2B collection infrastructure;
- launch/integration services;
- grants/hackathons;
- sponsorship/partnership revenue;
- permitted protocol fees.

Revenue and NFT market price must be treated as different things.
