# V22 Grant & Ecosystem Readiness

## Current Robinhood Chain opportunity

Robinhood publicly committed **$1 million** to the 2026 Arbitrum Open House program to support developer activity on Robinhood Chain testnet and future mainnet.

Robinhood Chain mainnet is now public and is positioned around tokenized assets, DeFi and AI-native applications.

TROLL's strongest grant narrative is therefore not "NFT collection."

It is:

> **A persistent, noncustodial NFT identity whose token-bound value biography can accumulate verified assets, preserve history across ownership transfer, separate unrestricted vault assets from regulated entitlements, and support human-authorized AI tooling.**

- full Robinhood testnet lifecycle proof;

- direct vault asset lane;

- agent-control architecture;
- V19 Slither baseline with zero High/Medium findings;
