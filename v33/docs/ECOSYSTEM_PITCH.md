# TROLL NFT 2.0 — Robinhood Chain Ecosystem Submission

## One sentence
TROLL NFT 2.0 is persistent NFT-linked identity and wallet infrastructure where a transferable NFT can preserve verifiable evolution, burn history, a protected token-ID-bound vault, separated regulated entitlements, and a machine-verifiable Passport across ownership changes.

## Why Robinhood Chain
Robinhood Chain is an EVM-compatible L2 designed for tokenized real-world assets and AI-native applications. TROLL's architecture is designed around exactly those primitives:
- persistent onchain identity;
- composable ERC-20 assets;
- clear separation of direct vault assets and regulated entitlements;
- noncustodial human-authorized agent tooling;
- public proof and analytics infrastructure.
